# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. A summary follows; the linked text is authoritative.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, welcoming and patient.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Trolling, personal attacks, or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without their permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow
this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate.

To report a problem, use [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/boss477/graphhacks/security/advisories/new)
or contact the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/boss477). Reports are
handled confidentially.
